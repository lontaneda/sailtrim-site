# Sailtrim Privacy Policy **Effective date: October 1, 2026** Sailtrim is an interactive sailing education app provided by **Oranda Studio**. This policy explains how the app handles information. ## Information stored on your device Sailtrim may save your settings and lesson progress on your device so they remain available when you reopen the app. We do not receive this information. Your device’s backup settings may affect whether local app data is included in a device backup. ## Information collected by the app Sailtrim does not require an account. The app does not collect or transmit personal information, location, sailing activity, or lesson progress to us. It does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs. ## Purchases If you purchase Sailtrim through Google Play, Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment card details. Google may provide us with purchase and transaction information needed to administer the app and address purchase issues. ## Contacting us If you email us for support, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use it to respond to your request and manage related support correspondence. Please do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to explain your issue. ## Data sharing and deletion We do not sell personal information or share information stored within Sailtrim with advertisers. You can remove locally stored app data through your device’s app settings or by uninstalling Sailtrim, subject to your device’s backup settings. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact us at **support@orandastudio.com**. ## Changes to this policy We may update this policy if Sailtrim’s features or data practices change. The current version will be available at this page with an updated effective date. ## Contact **Oranda Studio** **support@orandastudio.com**
